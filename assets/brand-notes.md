# KickoffWhen Brand Assets

## Concept

The mark combines three ideas:

- Crescent moon: late-night viewing for Asian time zones.
- Football as clock face: kickoff time is the core product promise.
- Green hand/spark: the moment of kickoff and the scheduling utility.

## Palette

- Midnight navy: `#0A1929`
- Moonlight yellow: `#FFD600`
- Pitch green: `#00C853`
- Panel white: `#F8FAFC`
- Muted slate: `#9FB2CA`

## Files

- `avatar-400.png`: 400x400 social avatar.
- `avatar-source.png`: original generated high-resolution source.
- `logo-mark.svg`: scalable square icon.
- `logo-horizontal.svg`: horizontal lockup for website headers and social banners.
